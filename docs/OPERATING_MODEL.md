# Personal Health Digital Twin Operating Model

## Daily Cycle

06:30
- collect authorized health data
- validate freshness and quality
- update health snapshot
- generate recommendations

## Weekly Cycle

Review:
- weight trend
- body fat trend
- muscle preservation
- training load
- sleep recovery
- nutrition adherence

## Decision Principles

1. Medical safety first
2. Latest valid data first
3. Trend over single measurement
4. Preserve muscle during fat loss
5. Explain recommendation evidence

## Data Priority

Medical laboratory data > clinician goals > wearable data > general guidance
